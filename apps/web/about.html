About Eigen Research

The work of diligence is separating what's proven from what's merely announced. Most of that work is reading.

Eigen Research does that reading. It answers questions about companies, markets and technologies from primary sources — regulatory filings, patents, research papers, public code and grant awards — and links every claim back to the document behind it. We built it because general-purpose AI tools sound equally confident whether or not they are right, and for a decision that moves real money you need to be able to check.

What we hold ourselves to

  • Primary sources only. No answer is assembled from a summary of a summary.
  • Announced is not proven. A press release and a patent application describe intent; an audited number and a granted patent describe what happened. We keep the two apart and label which you are reading.
  • Sentiment stays sentiment. Coverage tone and forum chatter are shown as market signal, never as established fact, and never as investment advice.
  • Explicit about the unknown. Where the evidence is not there, you get a stated gap instead of a confident guess. A gap is a finding, not a failure.
